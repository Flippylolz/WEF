# Product

This domain owns who the Warsaw Estate Platform serves, what the MVP does, and the user-visible quality and acceptance bar. Product requirement IDs remain stable and are defined exactly once in the experience document.

## Canonical documents

- [Scope](SCOPE.md) — product goal, audience, MVP boundaries, non-goals, and later candidates.
- [Experience](EXPERIENCE.md) — map, grouped pins, offers, filters, media, source links, authentication, and contact-reveal behavior.
- [Quality](QUALITY.md) — performance, accessibility, responsiveness, language, privacy/safety, and MVP acceptance.

## Interpretation

- “Imported” never means “currently available.”
- Publication dates are source dates and remain visible.
- Anonymous browsing is the default; username/password accounts gate restricted actions.
- The initial default map is Warsaw, while out-of-area source records remain importable.
- English ships first, with user-visible strings represented by i18n keys.

Product changes require testable acceptance criteria, traceability to affected epics/tasks, and a decision record when they alter confirmed behavior.
